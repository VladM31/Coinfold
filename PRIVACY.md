# Coinfold Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 17 October 2026 · [Українська версія](PRIVACY.uk.md)*

Coinfold ("the app") is an offline-first personal finance tracker for Android and iOS. This policy explains what data the app handles and what leaves your device. The short version: **your financial data stays on your phone**.

## 1. Data that stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored only in the app's local database on your device:

- accounts, balances and currencies;
- transactions, categories, notes, dates;
- recurring payments;
- settings, and (if you turn it on) a PIN.

We do not have servers, we do not create user accounts, and we cannot see, read or recover this data.

- **PIN.** Stored only as a salted, iterated hash on your device. It is never sent anywhere and is not included in backups.
- **Fingerprint / face unlock.** Handled entirely by your operating system. The app receives only a "success / failure" result, never biometric data.
- **Backups and exports.** A backup (JSON) or an export (CSV, PDF) is created only when you ask for it. The file is passed to the system share sheet, and you decide where it goes (a cloud drive, a messenger, e-mail). We do not receive these files.
- **Device backup.** If you have Google backup (Android) or iCloud backup (iOS) enabled on your device, your operating system may include the app's data in that backup. This is controlled by your device settings and the provider, not by us.

## 2. Data sent from your device

**Exchange rates.** To convert currencies the app downloads public exchange rates from the Monobank API (`api.monobank.ua`). The request contains no personal or financial data, but like any internet request it exposes your IP address to that service. See Monobank's own policy for how they handle it.

**Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase).** To find bugs and understand which features are used, the app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google. They may collect:

- anonymous usage events, for example "expense added", "transfer added", "backup created", "CSV exported", and which screens were opened;
- crash reports: the technical stack trace, app version, and device information (model, operating system version, language, free memory/storage, orientation);
- a random app-installation identifier generated by Firebase, and approximate location derived from the IP address (country/region level).

We **never** send amounts, balances, account or category names, notes, PINs or any other content you enter. We do not use the advertising ID and the app shows **no ads**. Data is processed by Google under its terms: <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>. Retention follows the Firebase defaults.

Analytics and crash reporting are disabled in development builds.

**Voice input.** If you use the microphone button when adding an expense, speech recognition is performed by the speech service installed on your device (for example Google). The app receives only the recognized text. Audio handling is governed by that provider's policy.

## 3. What we do not do

- We do not sell or rent data.
- We do not show ads or share data with advertisers.
- We do not build profiles and do not track you across other apps or websites.
- We do not collect your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

## 4. Your choices and rights

- All of your financial data is under your control: edit or delete it in the app, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
- To ask about, access, or delete analytics data associated with your installation, contact us (see below) or follow the step-by-step instructions in [DATA_DELETION.md](DATA_DELETION.md). Because the identifier is random, we may ask you for the installation identifier or other details to find it.
- If you are in the EU/EEA, UK or a similar region, you have the rights granted by applicable law (access, correction, deletion, objection, complaint to a supervisory authority).

## 5. Children

Coinfold is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.
